# Lab z/OS 28: Display your own RACF info

## Overview

You can view your RACF profile.

## Goals

- Use the LISTUSER command to view your RACF profile. 

## Part 1: Display your own RACF userid information

At the TSO READY prompt (not inside ISPF), enter the command LISTUSER <userid> TSO OMVS, where <userid> is your own userid. 

Read the information and see if you understand what it means.
